The Curriculum of Complexity

To understand why the Unitree G1 requires our complex, multi-loop tethered architecture, we must test it against 8 escalating scenarios, demonstrating exactly when and why standard paradigms catastrophically fail.

The 4 Evaluated Paradigms

1. Classical Robotics

ROS2 Nav2, 2D Lidar. Rigid, fast, zero semantic understanding.

2. End-to-End AI

VLA/VLM mapping pixels to action. High latency, black-box, physically unsafe.

3. Tethered Hybrid

Our 3-Loop split (Orin NX + RTX 5090). Balances safety, speed, and cognition.

4. "Thor" Future-State

Unlimited onboard compute running unquantized 34B models at 30Hz natively.

Level 1: Empty Room, Visible Goal (Line-of-Sight)

The Task: Walk 5 meters forward across an empty room to reach a visible blue marker.
  • ClassicalPass

    Uses simple PID control on camera centroids. Extremely fast, mathematically perfect.

  • End-to-End AIPass

    Maps pixels directly to walking gaits. High latency (~800ms) makes it wobbly, but it succeeds.

  • Tethered HybridPass

    VLM sees goal, MPPI executes a straight line safely.

Level 2: The Static Isolated Obstacle (The Chair)

The Task: Walk forward but maneuver around a semi-static chair in the path.
  • ClassicalPass

    2D Lidar sees the chair legs, Nav2 costmap inflates the obstacle, local planner routes around it.

  • End-to-End AIStruggle

    Network understands it's a chair, but high latency ruins precision foot placement. Frequently clips legs.

  • Tethered HybridPass

    RealSense + Elevation Grid maps the 3D geometry at 20Hz. MPPI routes the feet flawlessly.

Level 3: The Static Maze (Out of Line-of-Sight)

The Task: Navigate through a complex, multi-room office to find "Room 402".
  • ClassicalFail

    Fails entirely at semantic routing ("What is Room 402?"). Fails at 3D overhangs (tables, stairs) due to 2D Lidar.

  • End-to-End AIFail

    Lacks spatial object permanence. Without a persistent geometric map (SLAM), it wanders down dead ends and loops.

  • Tethered HybridPass

    3D Livox + AG-SLAM provides infinite memory. VLM performs semantic routing, MPPI follows the global SLAM path.

Level 4: The Unpredictable Dynamic Object

The Task: A dog suddenly runs directly in front of the robot's swinging foot.
  • ClassicalFail

    Nav2 local planners take ~100ms to react. The robot trips over the dog and falls.

  • End-to-End AICatastrophic Fail

    The ~800ms inference latency means the robot doesn't even realize the dog is there until it's already on the floor.

  • Tethered HybridPass

    The 500Hz CBF on the Orin NX acts as a mathematical safety floor, physically aborting the footstep in 2ms.

Level 5: The Semi-Static Environment

The Task: Traverse an area where a heavy door was opened and a couch was moved *after* the SLAM map was built.
  • ClassicalFail

    Blindly trusts the old map, refuses to enter the room. Creates permanent "ghost" pixels on the map if forced.

  • End-to-End AIPass

    Doesn't rely on a map; reacts to what it currently sees and walks through the open door.

  • Tethered HybridPass

    Epipolar Geometry Consistency instantly detects moved visual features, strips them from the Splat map, and organically inpaints.

Level 6: Sparse Crowd Navigation

The Task: Navigate a long hallway while 2-3 people walk in the opposite direction.
  • ClassicalFail

    Standard dynamic SLAM treats humans as moving brick walls. Robot stops constantly, violently recalculating paths.

  • End-to-End AIStruggle

    Can weave through semantically, but lacks precision footstep planning, making it look jerky and dangerous.

  • Tethered HybridPass

    YOLOv11-pose + LV-DOT explicitly tracks humans. MPPI predicts human trajectories, gracefully routing *behind* them.

Level 7: Dense Crowd with Flow (Conference Break)

The Task: Traverse a highly congested fair floor where attendees are moving in multiple opposing directions.
  • ClassicalCatastrophic Fail

    "Freezing Robot Problem". Costmap inflates around every human. The robot concludes there is 0% traversable space and freezes permanently.

  • End-to-End AIFail

    Hallucinates a path but cannot execute it safely in real-time, bumping into attendees.

  • Tethered HybridPass

    Dual-Pipeline Split masks the crowd. Swaps MPPI for π₀.₅ Flow Matching to learn the "fluid dynamics" of the crowd and weave natively.

Level 8: Social Navigation & Interaction (Final Boss)

The Task: Navigate the fair, identify an interested person, approach them, and hand them a flyer.
  • ClassicalComplete Failure

    Has absolutely zero concept of social dynamics, gaze, or human intent.

  • End-to-End AIStruggle

    Can identify an interested person, but lacks architectural flexibility to run simultaneous dialogue models and safety planners.

  • Tethered HybridPass

    Cosmos VLM parses Gaze at 1Hz. RTX 5090 triggers Context Swap to load Omni-LLM. Robot routes to person (MPPI), raises arm, initiates dialogue, all while 500Hz CBF ensures it doesn't step on toes.

Conclusion

By scaling the complexity, we definitively prove that a pure classical stack fails at Level 3 (Cognition), a pure AI stack fails at Level 4 (Physics/Latency), but the Tethered Hybrid Architecture scales perfectly through Level 8 by assigning high-latency semantics to the GPU cloud, and low-latency survival physics to the edge.
