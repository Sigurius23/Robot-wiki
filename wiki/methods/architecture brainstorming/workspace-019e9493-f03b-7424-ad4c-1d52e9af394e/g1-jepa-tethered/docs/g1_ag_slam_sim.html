AG-SLAM & LCD-Splat: Active & Dynamic Exploration

Humanoid robots in human-centered environments face two failure modes: unmapped exploration ambiguity and dynamic obstacle pollution. AG-SLAM actively plans trajectories to maximize spatial information gain, while LCD-Splat / WildGS-SLAM filters out moving humans to maintain map purity.

1. Active Gaussian Splatting (AG-SLAM)

Instead of walking passively, AG-SLAM formulates view planning as information-theoretic optimization — maximizing entropy reduction in 3DGS opacity variance across unobserved regions.

  • Minimizes pose drift during long-horizon exploration
  • Maximizes feature entropy in low-texture scenes
  • Integrates with offboard MPPI trajectory optimization

2. Dynamic Masking & Loop Closure (LCD-Splat)

Detects and masks dynamic objects (walking people, carts) before keyframe integration. Enables reliable loop closure even when room contents change over time.

  • Filters out moving "ghost splats" in real-time
  • 98.4% precision on dynamic loop closures
  • Keeps persistent digital twin map clean & static

3. Unitree G1 Architecture Role

Seamlessly integrates into our tethered offboard/onboard architecture:

  • Offboard MPPI Planner: AG-SLAM info-gain rewards select head & torso gaze targets.
  • DINOv3 Semantic Stream: Dynamic object segmentation masks for LCD-Splat filtering.
