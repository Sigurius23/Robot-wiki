🧠 Ideal Crowd Navigation Data-Flow Stack

End-to-End compute pipeline optimized for the Unitree G1 EDU (16GB Jetson Orin NX) and an Offboard RTX laptop.

Robot Head Assembly

1. Multi-Sensor Perception

Raw environmental data ingestion.

Livox Mid-360 3D LiDAR 10 Hz

Omnidirectional non-repetitive Rhodonea-curve scanning.

RealSense D435i RGB-D 60 Hz

Active stereo depth and global-shutter RGB.

6-Axis IMU 200 Hz

High-frequency angular velocity & acceleration.

Jetson Orin NX (16GB RAM)

2. Onboard Tracking

Local state estimation & identity tracking.

FAST-LIO2 (CPU) 10 Hz

Deskews point clouds via IEKF.

YOLOv11-pose (GPU) 60 FPS

Extracts 17 COCO keypoints (TensorRT).

LV-DOT / AEKF (CPU) 30 FPS

Fuses 3D/2D tracking + velocity vectors.

Dynamic Masking (CPU) 10 Hz

Splits Dynamic (Humans) from Static (Room).

Offboard Laptop (RTX 5090)

3. Mapping & Routing

Global map generation and semantic intent.

AG-SLAM / LCD-Splat 10 Hz

Builds semantic 3D Gaussian map of the static env.

Social Intent & Interaction 1 Hz

Cosmos/PaliGemma classify "approachable" vs "busy".

π₀.₅ Flow Routing 40 Hz

Routes TO interested people, AVOIDS busy crowds.

Omni-LLM / GR00T 20 Hz

Real-time Audio-Visual dialogue & manipulation.

G1 Motor Controllers

4. Execution & Safety

Translation to physical torques.

CBF Safety Floor 500 Hz

Overrides VLA if LiDAR SDF < 0.3m.

Whole Body Control 500 Hz

Converts trajectories to 43-DoF joint torques.
