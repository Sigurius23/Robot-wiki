LiDAR Literature & Architecture

Synthesizing the core LiDAR literature (FAST-LIO2, GPS-SLAM, MinkowskiNet) and why the G1 humanoid's tethered 2026 architecture prioritizes deterministic geometry over neural rendering for physical safety.

⚠️ The "Stability Decay" Problem

The G1's LIVOX MID-360 provides 360° horizontal coverage but only a 59° vertical field of view, creating a large blind spot behind the robot's heels. Using pure autoregressive V-JEPA or VLA models to step backward is highly dangerous because they hallucinate ("decay") unobserved geometry. Persistent LiDAR-inertial maps (via FAST-LIO2 + CBF) guarantee true metric occupancy safety.

📚 Selected LiDAR Literature

The mathematical foundations establishing LiDAR as the primary metric sensor for our embodied AI stack.

FAST-LIO2: Fast Direct Odometry IEEE T-RO 2022

Direct raw-point registration using an iterated Kalman filter. Operates independently of scene texture, providing a 200 Hz state estimate natively on the Orin NX.

● Role: Primary Metric State Estimation
GPS-SLAM: SDF + 3DGS Fusion IROS 2025

LiDAR-Augmented Gaussian Signed Distance Field Mapping. Fuses continuous metric SDFs (for collision checking) with sparse on-demand 3D Gaussians (for VLA semantic inputs).

● Role: Unified Safety & Rendering Map
MinkowskiNet 4D Spatiotemporal CVPR 2019

Sparse convolution backbone. Used in our architecture to ingest temporal sequences of LiDAR points into a 4D latent representation for the LeJEPA World Model.

● Role: Latent Voxel Encoding (2 GB VRAM)
Depth Anything V2 vs. LiDAR MATEC 2025

Empirical evaluation proving SOTA monocular depth estimation lacks the sub-millimeter reliability required for indoor avoidance, cementing the need for hardware LiDAR.

● Role: Architecture Justification Matrix
