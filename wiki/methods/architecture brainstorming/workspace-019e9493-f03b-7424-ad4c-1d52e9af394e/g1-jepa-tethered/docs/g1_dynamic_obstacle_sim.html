🛡️ The "Safety Onion": Crash Avoidance Architecture

Multi-layered dynamic obstacle avoidance for dense pedestrian environments (e.g. Conference Halls).

1. Architecture Layers

The G1 avoids collisions by nesting three time-domain safety loops. High-level routing prevents dense clusters, mid-level MPC avoids walking paths, and low-level CBF enforces absolute metric safety.

2. Layer Specifications

L1: Semantic Social Navigation

1 Hz • Offboard

Global path planning through crowds. Uses YOLOv11-pose and LV-DOT (LiDAR-Visual Dynamic Obstacle Tracking) to fuse RealSense D435i RGB-D and Livox Mid-360 data to assess crowd density and social intent.

YOLOv11-pose LV-DOT DAL (Fusion)

🧍 Crowd Pose & Intent Tracking (AEKF)

Uses YOLOv11-pose via TensorRT to extract 17 COCO keypoints from pedestrians at 60 FPS.

• Calculates Body Heading Angle via shoulder/hip vectors.
• Calculates Head Gaze Angle via facial normals.
• Fuses kinematics via an Adaptive Extended Kalman Filter (AEKF) to predict if they will walk into the robot's path.

L2: Predictive Trajectory Avoidance

40 Hz • Offboard

As pedestrians walk, the 4D Voxel Memory extracts their velocity vectors. The Model Predictive Path Integral (MPPI) or Flow VLA continuously curves the robot's trajectory to glide around them smoothly without stopping.

4D-NVS MPPI π₀.₅ Flow

L3: Reactive Safety Floor (Fail-Safe)

500 Hz • Onboard

The neural networks are bypassed entirely. If a pedestrian suddenly steps backward within 0.3 meters of the robot, the onboard LiDAR Signed Distance Field (SDF) triggers an invariant Control Barrier Function (CBF).

Orin NX FAST-LIO2 CBF WBC

⛔ Hard Collision Avoidance

Even if the VLM predicts the person is stationary, physics trumps semantics. The CBF instantly commands the Whole-Body Controller (WBC) to execute a kinematic braking maneuver or postural shift to maintain distance.
